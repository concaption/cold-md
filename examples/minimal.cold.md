## Value

We [do X specific thing] for [specific audience] so they [specific outcome].

## Voice

### Do
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Name a concrete number or tool in the first line.
- Close with one yes/no question.

### Don't
- Open with pleasantries.
- Use em dashes.
- Name-drop unless it solves their problem.

## Proof

- [Real number 1]
- [Real client or outcome 2]
- [Specific case study or link 3]

## Sequence

### 1. Opener (Day 0)
- Subject: 5 words max, lowercase.
- Body: 40-60 words.
- Reference one observable fact about their company.
- Ask one yes/no question.

### 2. Bump (Day 3)
- Subject: "Re: " + original.
- Body: 20 words max.
- Offer one new piece of value.
- No question.

### 3. Breakup (Day 7)
- Subject: "Last one".
- Body: 15 words max.
- Soft goodbye, no CTA.

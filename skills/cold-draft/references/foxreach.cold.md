# FoxReach - cold.md

The actual cold.md FoxReach uses for its own outbound. Lifted straight from production. If you want a reference implementation of this spec, this is it.

## Identity

I'm Usama, founder of FoxReach. We build cold email infrastructure that doesn't torch your domain - warmup, reply triage, booked-calls dashboard. Before FoxReach I ran outbound for 40+ B2B clients across Upwork. I care about this stack because I've broken it myself.

## Audience

- B2B SaaS and lead-gen agencies, 5-50 employees
- Title: Head of Growth, Head of Demand Gen, Founder, Outbound Lead
- Currently sending cold email at volume (Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo, or in-house)
- Managing 3+ sending domains or 10+ inboxes
- English-speaking markets (US, UK, CA, AU, EU)

### Not for
- Solo founders without an outbound motion yet (we're overkill)

## Value

FoxReach warms your inboxes, routes replies through AI triage, and hands you a booked-calls dashboard - for teams managing 5+ sending domains.

## Voice

### Do
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Name a specific tool, inbox count, or domain in the first line.
- Reference their sending volume or stack if observable.
- Close with one yes/no question.
- Lowercase subject lines.

### Don't
- Open with pleasantries ("Hope you're well," etc.).
- Use em dashes. Regular hyphens with spaces only.
- Name-drop clients who aren't relevant to their exact stack.
- Pitch pricing in the first message.
- Use the word "solution."

## Sequence

### 1. Opener (Day 0)
- Subject: 5 words max, lowercase, no buzzwords.
- Body: 40-60 words.
- Reference one observable fact about their outbound (stack, volume, domain count).
- Ask one yes/no question about deliverability or reply rate.

### 2. Bump (Day 3)
- Subject: "Re: " + original opener subject.
- Body: 20 words max.
- Offer a free artifact: deliverability audit, warmup benchmark, or reply-triage demo.
- No question.

### 3. Case study (Day 7)
- Subject: lowercase, names a comparable company or tool.
- Body: 60 words max.
- One before/after number from FoxReach proof list.
- One yes/no question.

### 4. Breakup (Day 12)
- Subject: "last one on this".
- Body: 15 words max.
- Offer to close the loop, door open.
- No CTA.

## Objections

> "We already use Instantly / Smartlead / Apollo."

Great - most of our best clients came from one of those. We sit on top: warmup that actually builds reputation, reply triage that sorts interested/not-now/OOO, and a booked-calls view across every sending domain. Want a 5-minute teardown of your current setup?

> "We built this in-house."

Respect. Most in-house systems break at the 10-domain mark - warmup gets noisy and triage gets manual. If you want a second pair of eyes on what you've built, the deliverability audit is free and takes 10 minutes: https://foxreach.io/audit.

> "Not interested."

No problem. If things shift, the audit is free and one-shot: https://foxreach.io/audit. Best of luck with Q2.

> "Send more info."

Happy to, but faster if I know what you care about - deliverability, reply rates, or the triage layer. Which one?

> "What does it cost?"

Depends on domain count. For 5 domains, we start at a flat monthly. For 20+, it's per-domain. Want me to send the two-line pricing note?
